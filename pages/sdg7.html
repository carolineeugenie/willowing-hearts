Affordable & Clean Energy

Access to reliable and sustainable energy is essential for improving lives and supporting economic growth. Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 7: Affordable and Clean Energy aims to ensure that everyone has access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy. Clean energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydropower help reduce pollution, combat climate change, and create a healthier environment for future generations. Achieving this goal requires innovation, investment, and responsible energy use. Individuals can contribute by conserving electricity, using energy-efficient appliances, and supporting renewable energy initiatives. Governments and businesses also play a key role in expanding access to clean energy technologies. By working together, we can create a future where energy is accessible to all while protecting our planet for generations to come.
