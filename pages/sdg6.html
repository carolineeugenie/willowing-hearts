Clean Water & Sanitation

Clean water and proper sanitation are essential for healthy lives and sustainable communities. Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 6: Clean Water and Sanitation aims to ensure that everyone has access to safe drinking water, adequate sanitation, and proper hygiene. Despite progress, millions of people around the world still face water shortages and lack basic sanitation facilities, putting their health and well-being at risk. Protecting water resources is a shared responsibility. Conserving water, reducing pollution, and practicing good hygiene are simple actions that can make a lasting impact. Governments, communities, and individuals must work together to improve water management and sanitation systems for everyone. By valuing and protecting this vital resource, we can create a healthier, more sustainable future for generations to come.
